About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Southampton, United Kingdom. I completed my PhD in Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, India.

My research focuses on solid mechanics, advanced structural systems, mechanical metamaterials, finite element modelling, and artificial intelligence-assisted engineering design.

Research Interests

Mechanical Metamaterials

Architected lattice materials, structural behaviour, and lightweight engineering systems.

Computational Mechanics

Finite element analysis, structural modelling, and numerical simulation.

AI and Machine Learning

Data-driven modelling and intelligent design of engineered materials.
