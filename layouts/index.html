Dr. Bhargav Reddy Isanaka

Research Fellow | University of Southampton

I am fascinated by how structures and engineered materials behave under different loading conditions and real-world applications. Understanding the underlying mechanics that govern their behaviour, exploring their response to complex loading environments and identifying ways to enhance their performance are what drive my interest in engineering research.

I am currently a Research Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering at the University of Southampton, UK. I hold a PhD in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, India.

About Me

My interests lie in understanding and exploiting the mechanics of structural and material behaviour to address engineering challenges. I am particularly interested in how geometry, material properties, structural configurations and loading conditions influence mechanical performance.

My work spans architected metamaterials, lattice structures, computational mechanics, structural analysis and sustainable construction materials. I enjoy bringing together numerical modelling, experimental investigations and artificial intelligence to explore engineering solutions and improve structural performance.
