About Me

I am a Research Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering at the University of Southampton, UK. I completed my PhD in Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu.

My academic interests include architected metamaterials, lattice structures, computational mechanics, structural analysis and sustainable construction materials. I am particularly interested in combining numerical simulations, experimental validation and machine learning to improve structural performance.
