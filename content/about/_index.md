I am an engineering researcher specialising in computational mechanics, advanced materials and structural behaviour, with research experience spanning sustainable construction materials, finite element analysis and architected materials. My work focuses on understanding how material composition, structural geometry and loading conditions influence mechanical performance, and on developing computational and data-driven approaches to improve engineering design.

I am currently a **Research Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering at the University of Southampton, UK**, working on the dynamics and multifunctional behaviour of lattice-based mechanical metamaterials. I hold a PhD in Civil Engineering from the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jammu** and an M.Tech in Structural Engineering from **Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Anantapur (JNTUA)**.

## Research Expertise

My research brings together mechanics-based modelling, numerical simulation, machine learning and experimental investigations. My principal areas of interest include:

- **Architected Materials and Mechanical Metamaterials:** Lattice structures, honeycomb architectures, Voronoi networks, geometry-driven mechanical performance and multifunctional design.
- **Computational Mechanics:** Nonlinear finite element analysis, structural modelling, deformation mechanisms, failure analysis and numerical simulation.
- **Machine Learning for Engineering:** Surrogate modelling, Gaussian Process Regression, Decision Trees and explainable AI for material and structural design.
- **Structural Dynamics:** Vibration analysis, dynamic response, wave propagation and experimental characterisation of lattice-based structures.
- **Construction and Sustainable Materials:** High-performance concrete, mineral admixtures, mechanical properties and durability.

## Selected Research Contributions

### Geometry-driven Design of Lattice Metamaterials

Investigated curved honeycomb architectures and reinforced Voronoi lattices to improve stiffness, strength, deformation control and energy absorption through geometric modifications and reinforcement strategies.

### Machine-learning-assisted Structural Enhancement

Developed data-driven approaches to identify critical failure regions, guide selective strengthening and predict mechanical properties and stress–strain responses of lattice structures.

### Computational Analysis of Structural Systems

Investigated the static and free-vibration behaviour of thin plates with curvilinear stiffeners using finite element analysis, alongside earlier research on high-performance concrete incorporating combinations of mineral admixtures.

### Research Translation and Funded Project Experience

Contributed to research associated with an ARDB–DRDO-funded project on lightweight multifunctional metastructures, connecting computational investigations of architected materials with potential engineering applications.

## Current Research Direction

At the University of Southampton, my research is extending towards the dynamic and multifunctional performance of lattice-based mechanical metamaterials. The broader research themes include:

- Design of multifunctional mechanical metamaterials.
- Dynamic and wave propagation analysis of smart structures and lattices.
- Experimental characterisation of dynamic metamaterials.
- Integration of computational and experimental approaches for advanced structural design.

I am particularly interested in how engineered geometry and material architecture can be exploited to tailor structural response under static and dynamic loading conditions.
